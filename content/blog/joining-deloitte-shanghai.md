I am joining **Deloitte** in Shanghai as an **Analyst 1 on Technology Transformation**.

That sentence is simple. The path to it was not: WashU for an M.S. in Computer Science, backend work that had to stay up, research code that had to be reproducible, and a portfolio that tried to feel like a product rather than a résumé dump.

## What I am optimizing for

Consulting will change the unit of work. Instead of owning a service or a model end-to-end, the job is to help clients **modernize systems**, **ship change**, and **make technology actually land**.

What I want to keep sharp:

1. **Reliable backends** — clear contracts, boring failure modes, measurable uptime.
2. **Applied ML / CV** — not demo notebooks; pipelines that survive real data.
3. **Taste** — interfaces and writing that respect the reader’s time.
